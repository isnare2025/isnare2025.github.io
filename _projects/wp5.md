WP5 integrates the data generated across the pipeline and turns it into comparable, quantitative conclusions.

Responsibilities:

- Integration of multi-scale datasets from in silico, in vitro and in vivo studies.
- Machine learning, dimensionality reduction and Bayesian modeling of treatment effects.
- Quantitative comparison of the efficacy and potency of all candidate treatments.
- Identification of pre- and post-treatment biomarkers for drug-response prediction, target engagement and therapeutic effect.
- Validation of candidate biomarkers against available quantitative patient EEG data.
