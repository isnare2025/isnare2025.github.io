WP1 coordinates the consortium and keeps the integrated pipeline of in vitro and in vivo assays standardised across partners, so that data generated in different laboratories can be compared directly.

Responsibilities:

- Scientific and administrative coordination of iSNARE.
- Maintenance of the standardised, integrated framework of assays used in WP2–WP5.
- Alignment of candidate selection with the three phases of development (replication study, emerging candidates, de novo compounds).
- Links to the national treatment sites in nine EU countries and Israel, so that promising results reach clinical practice rapidly.
- Exchange with the patient advocacy organisations participating in iSNARE.
