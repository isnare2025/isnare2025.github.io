WP2 designs novel candidate compounds from scratch. In silico modeling combines artificial intelligence and physics-based technologies with the latest algorithms to support both structure-based and ligand-based approaches.

Responsibilities:

- Modeling of validated SNARE protein templates to identify druggable sites.
- Design of novel compounds for the third phase of the iSNARE development pipeline.
- Prioritisation of candidates for experimental testing in WP3 and WP4.
- Integration of in silico predictions with the experimental results produced by the other work packages.
