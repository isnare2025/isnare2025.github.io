WP3 assesses candidate treatments in human neurons. It builds on the patient-own iPSC-derived SNAREopathy cell models already generated by consortium partners, including scalable single neuron cultures and GABAergic/glutamatergic co-cultures.

Responsibilities:

- Generation, characterisation and automation of patient-own iPSC-derived neuron assays.
- Measurement of established cellular phenotypes before and after treatment.
- Testing of all three candidate phases within the standardised framework.
- Application of innovative compound delivery methods specifically targeted to the brain.
