WP4 tests candidate treatments in vivo. The consortium capitalises on the SNAREopathy mouse models developed over two decades by its partners, whose characterisation produced robust phenotypes that mirror the human condition.

Responsibilities:

- Treatment studies in validated SNAREopathy mouse models, focused on _STXBP1_, _SYT1_ and _SNAP25_.
- Quantitative EEG recordings before and after treatment to derive candidate biomarkers.
- Behavioural and molecular read-outs aligned with the in vitro assays of WP3.
- Delivery of comparable, quantitative data sets to WP5.
