## Patient advocacy organisations

Patient organisations are full partners in iSNARE: they drive registry development, collect real-world evidence, organise the patient family meetings and keep the research questions anchored in the lived experience of families.

### Rafa's Moonshot

Rafa's Moonshot is dedicated to accelerating research and therapeutic development for STXBP1-related disorders and other SNAREopathies. Founded by the parents of an affected child, it acts as a bridge between the scientific community and families. Its contributions to iSNARE focus on patient-centred research — patient registry development, real-world data collection and patient family meetings — and on investigating β-Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN) as a potential therapeutic for STXBP1 disorders, including the development of a transgenic STXBP1-R292C mouse model.
